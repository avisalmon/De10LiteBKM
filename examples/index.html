How every example is made

  • examples/NN_name/src/ the Verilog; sim/ the unit tests; index.html the page; <name>.qar (no prebuilt .sof: build it, a few minutes).
  • Shared, tested modules in lib/rtl/ (display chain, PLL, RAMs, ROMs, SPI, SDRAM, accelerometer, USB text link...); the board's pins in lib/board/de10_lite_pins.csv.
  • One command per step in scripts/: new_project.py (project from the top module's ports), de10.py (build, program, flash, pack, log, screen, cam), sim.py (Icarus or ModelSim), make_stp.py (Signal Tap).
