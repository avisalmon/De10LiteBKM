Reading: with one unit the consumer is the limit (T 1 takes one word a clock, the channel refills while it waits
for the write-back); from two units up the SDRAM bus is the limit at 98 % with bursts of 1024, and each unit is
starved in proportion (6 units: 84 % waiting, 16 % computing) because the bus is shared six ways.

bus over 100 % on the 64 x 64 job: clocks is the longest unit's busy span, and the bus is busy a little before
the first unit starts (filling the FIFOs) and after the last one is done (the lanes' write-back tail), so on a
job of a few thousand clocks the bus counts more clocks than the units' span. From ~10,000 clocks up it is the true bus use.
