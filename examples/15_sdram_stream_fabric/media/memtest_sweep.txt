--- reading the two maps together ---
Each rd_delay has one passing window per period. Build A's rd_delay-4 window was +17..+1 across the wrap (-7..+1 and
+17..+24), i.e. the compiled phase sat 1 step (~300 ps) from the window's edge, where the chip's command-sampling edge
moves to the next clock. Moving the compiled phase by 6875 ps (= 33 steps, the window centre) puts rd_delay 4 in the
middle of its window: -16..+16 steps = +-3.3 ns margin at 100 MHz. rd_delay 3 works one command clock earlier (window
+19..+27 in build B), rd_delay 5 never: the read data of this chip arrives 4 clocks after the READ at 100 MHz.
